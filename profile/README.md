> **SciEra — From Scientific Discovery to True AGI.**
>
> We believe **Science** is the most vital and central piece of the AGI puzzle. The era to come will belong to **SciEra**.
>
> Looking back at the trajectory of artificial intelligence—from early NLP and CV to LLMs, MLLMs, Embodied AI, Agents, and Physical Intelligence—these monumental waves of innovation are **merely the prologue**: teaching machines to perceive the world, understand language, and act within it. At their core, they still compress humanity's past experiences and mirror the surface of the known world.
>
> True AGI does not stop at imitating what humanity already knows. It lies in **unveiling the underlying laws of the universe and charting the causal boundaries of the unknown**. Only when machines can formulate hypotheses, reason rigorously, falsify assumptions, and discover new laws like scientists will intelligence truly possess the power to transcend the ceiling of human cognition.
>
> *From fitting the past to creating the future. From mimicking appearances to exploring essence.*
>
> **Welcome to SciEra.**
